About this page

Information, not a storefront

This page provides general health and safety information only. It is not medical advice, a pharmacy, or a source of medicines. No products are offered, sold, or supplied, and no orders are accepted.

Put your health first

Talk with a qualified health professional.

Medicines and substances obtained without appropriate medical supervision may be counterfeit, contaminated, incorrectly labelled, or unsuitable for you. A doctor or pharmacist can discuss your individual health, possible risks, and lawful treatment options.

Understand the risks

Categories of substances

Peptides

Products marketed online may not be authorised medicines and may have uncertain quality. Do not use them to diagnose or treat a condition without medical advice.

Testosterone and anabolic steroids

Hormone medicines require appropriate clinical assessment and monitoring. Unsupervised use can carry serious health risks; seek advice from a qualified clinician.

SARMs

Products sold as SARMs may be unapproved medicines with uncertain ingredients and safety. Avoid self-treatment and discuss concerns with a doctor or pharmacist.

Other medicines

Use prescription medicines only as directed by the prescribing professional. Do not share medicines or change treatment without consulting your care team.
